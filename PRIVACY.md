We may update this policy if we add new online features. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do.

## 8. Discord-specific disclosure

This section is included for **Discord application verification** and for users who want detail on our Discord integration.

### 8.1 What Llama 97 OS is on Discord

Llama 97 OS uses a **Discord application** registered with Discord primarily for **Rich Presence** via Discord’s desktop RPC interface. The Service is **not** a Discord bot that:

- joins servers or channels
- reads message content
- accesses voice channels
- lists guild members
- sends messages on your behalf

### 8.2 What data flows to Discord

When Rich Presence is enabled and the Discord desktop client is running, the app may send **activity metadata** to the Discord client, such as:

- Application identity (our Discord application ID)
- That you are using Llama 97 OS
- Optional status details (for example a short state or detail string about in-app activity)
- Timestamps related to how long you have been in that activity

That information is handled by **Discord** according to [Discord’s Privacy Policy](https://discord.com/privacy) and your Discord account settings. We do not receive a copy of your Discord messages, friends list, or server list through this integration.

### 8.3 What we do not do with Discord today

- We do **not** use Discord OAuth or the Discord API to log you into Llama 97 OS.
- We do **not** store Discord user IDs, tokens, or refresh tokens on LunaLucid servers.
- We do **not** request privileged gateway intents or access message content.
- We do **not** sell or rent Discord-related data.

### 8.4 Your choices

- Quit Discord, or disable activity sharing in Discord’s settings, to stop Rich Presence from appearing.
- Uninstall Llama 97 OS to stop the app from sending Rich Presence updates.

If we later add Discord login, invites, or other Discord APIs, we will update this Privacy Policy and our Terms of Service **before** those features ship.
